# HACKER PROTOCOL: CYBER WORLD — Project Plan

## Vision
A 3D hybrid cyberpunk world combining a physical city, an explorable cyberspace, a customizable personal hacker lab, fictional puzzle-based hacking, story missions, and multiplayer modes.

## Locked design
- Hybrid physical + digital world
- Customizable and upgradeable hacker lab
- Walking, sprinting, jumping, parkour, driving and exploration (phased)
- Third-person + first-person camera
- Offline story/core gameplay plus online shared-world and private sessions
- Android-first, Godot 4.x

## Development principle
Build a small, complete playable slice before expanding the city or adding large-scale multiplayer.

## Milestones
- v0.1: 3D lab blockout, movement, camera, workstation puzzle, Android APK
- v0.2: mobile touch controls, lab interaction and local save
- v0.3: lab customization and upgrades
- v0.4: cyberspace simulation level
- v0.5: first Nova City district and NPCs
- v0.6: vehicles and story missions
- v0.7: backend accounts and cloud save
- v0.8: private multiplayer sessions
- v0.9: shared-world test
- v1.0: optimized public beta

## Safety
All hacking mechanics are fictional simulations and puzzles. No real-world unauthorized access functionality is included.
